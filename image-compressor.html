Open image compressor

How it works

Select one or more images, choose the output format and quality, then download the processed files.

Supported formats

JPG, PNG and WebP. PNG encoding remains lossless in standard browser canvas output.

Privacy

The tool is designed to process selected image data locally in your browser rather than uploading the image to an application server.
